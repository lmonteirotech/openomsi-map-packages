# Credits

The packages in this repository are worked out from **maps made by other people**. The maps, their timetables and everything in their files
belong to their authors; this repository holds only what was worked out from them (lines, stops, times, shapes of land), and none of the maps' files.
**Credit the map and its authors whenever you share a package.**

## Maps

### Berlin-Spandau
The stock map of **OMSI 2**, by the developers of OMSI 2 (Marcel Kuhnt / MR-Software; published by Aerosoft). Every owner of OMSI 2 has it.
The water, green land, rivers, rail, districts, streets, stations and relief of its package come from OpenStreetMap (see below).

### Projekt Szczecin (4.0.1)
By **mpaw82** and **tomass94**, with the cooperation of **Kruchu**, **Darek59** and **Nick™**; the readme was translated by Translator.
Models and add-ons made for the map: Kruchu (bus stops and shelters, railway viaduct, buildings, RTPI displays, the Gocław traffic control
room, tram Konstal 105N), tomass94 (Sybiraków Roundabout, streets and the Wyzwolenia / Malczewskiego / Matejki square), Darek59
(Kołłątaja terminus, Giedroycia Roundabout), Nick™ (the "Stocznia Szczecińska" control room). See the readme that comes with the map for the
rest of its credits and its licence terms.

### Fikcyjny Szczecin (2.50.02, 18.07.2016)
By **MrYanke$** (MrYankess, www.facebook.com/MrYankess). Thanks of the author, as in the map's own readme: KaJotT3D and skawek (tests),
Sebastian (tests and motivation), Szofeero and TranslatorPS (timetables and duties), solari-adi / vPixel (the more than 300 stop announcements),
Lion's and Siroccoxp (objects and splines from Wrocław), TranslatorPS (translation), Allison (Szczecin liveries for AI vehicles),
Solaris_U12 (the "StationLinks" option), Szaqql (splines and a grass texture), DeadRussia (who motivated the author to go on).
The author allows the map to be published on www.Strefa-OMSI.pl and www.omnibussimulator.de and **forbids modifying it**: the package does not
modify the map, it is a separate folder that OMSI 2 itself ignores.

## Data

- **OpenStreetMap**: the data taken from it (water, green land, rivers, rail, borders, districts, streets, relief of Berlin-Spandau; the shore of
  Projekt Szczecin) is (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, under the ODbL 1.0 ([LICENSE-DATA.md](LICENSE-DATA.md)). Fikcyjny Szczecin has none.
- **Elevation**: the relief of Berlin-Spandau comes from the public Terrarium elevation tiles (built from SRTM and other models), read by `tools/mapgen/relief.py`.
